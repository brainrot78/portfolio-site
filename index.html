01 / About

Engineer first,
analyst always.

I'm a Cybersecurity Engineer (ESPRIT, class of 2026) and I currently work in the Security Operations Center at INTERPOL in Lyon. My work sits where security operations meet engineering: triaging and investigating alerts, then turning repetitive analyst work into tested, documented automation.

I started out in penetration testing and phishing simulation, then moved into threat intelligence and vulnerability management in banking and finance. Along the way I built a CTI platform that surfaced exposures 14 days ahead of traditional scanners, and a vulnerability management platform covering 640+ assets.

Outside work I build full labs end to end. My favourite is a bank-like infrastructure with layered firewalls, a WAF, honeypots, EDR, threat intelligence and SOAR, all wired together. I write about what I learn on the blog.
